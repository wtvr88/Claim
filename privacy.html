Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 4 September 2025

CF Claims Ltd (“we”, “us” or “our”) operates this website to help motorists reclaim compensation for mis‑sold car finance. We take your privacy seriously and comply with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations 2003 (PECR). This policy explains how we collect, use, store and share personal data when you visit our site or use our services.

1. Definitions

Personally identifiable information (PII) refers to any information that identifies you, such as your name, address, phone number, email address, financial details or IP address.

Electronic communications include emails, SMS, push notifications and calls used for marketing purposes under PECR.

2. Legal Basis for Processing

We only collect and process personal data when we have a lawful basis to do so. The legal bases we rely on include:

  • Consent – particularly for marketing emails and SMS.
  • Legitimate interests – such as improving our services or preventing fraud, provided these do not override your rights.
  • Contractual necessity – when processing is required to provide our services to you.

3. PECR and Direct Marketing

Under PECR, we only send electronic marketing communications with your explicit opt‑in consent unless the soft opt‑in exemption applies (for example, promoting similar services to existing customers). We will always tell you how your contact details will be used when you provide them and include a clear unsubscribe option in every marketing message. We never purchase third‑party marketing lists.

4. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Basic profile data such as your name and contact details.
  • Information provided by you through our contact form, including your enquiry details.
  • Payment and transactional information if you become a client.
  • User preferences and cookie data (see section 5).
  • IP addresses and technical data from your browser or device.

5. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse how our site is used and deliver relevant content. Where these cookies are not strictly necessary, we request your consent via a cookie banner.

We use the following categories of cookies:

  • Strictly necessary cookies – essential to make the site work.
  • Performance cookies – help us understand how visitors use our site.
  • Functionality cookies – remember your preferences.
  • Marketing cookies – used to personalise advertising (only with your consent).

6. How We Use Personal Data

We use your data to provide and improve our services, facilitate transactions, communicate with you, conduct analytics and comply with legal obligations. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

7. Sharing of Personal Data

We may share data with trusted service providers (for example, solicitors, payment processors and marketing platforms) who process data on our behalf. We require them to adhere to strict data protection standards. We may also share your data with regulators or law enforcement if required by law.

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encryption, access controls based on job role and regular security reviews.

9. International Transfers

If your data is transferred outside the UK or European Economic Area, we use standard contractual clauses or other safeguards to ensure adequate protection.

10. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, withdraw consent, and complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). To exercise these rights, contact us at info@cfclaims.example.

11. Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes we collected it for. When it’s no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it in accordance with our retention policy.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the updated version on this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email or on the site.
